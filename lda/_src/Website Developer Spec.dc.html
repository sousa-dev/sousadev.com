01 — SCOPE & SITEMAP

Seven pages, one action.

/ — Home (designed)
/services — 4 services, one section each
/process — How we work, FAQ
/products — 6 product cards → external
/about — Founder, team, location
/insights — Blog index · /insights/[slug]
/contact — Proposal form (same as home CTA)
/privacy — Legal (RGPD)
/pt/… — mirror of every route
Every page ends with the proposal CTA block. Same component, same copy. The form posts to a serverless endpoint (Resend or Formspree) and sends to hello@sousadev.com; success state replaces the form inline.
Secondary pages reuse homepage sections. /services expands the 4 service cards into full sections; /process is the 4 steps plus an FAQ accordion; /products is the 6-card grid with filters (Live / In development). No new visual patterns are needed.
Out of scope for v1: case studies (no data yet), careers, client portal, dark/light toggle (site is dark-first only, with the Process section in Paper as designed).

Astro 4, static output. Deploy to Cloudflare Pages or Vercel. No React/Vue islands unless a component truly needs state — only the FAQ accordion and the mobile nav, both doable with a native details element and a 20-line script.
Fonts self-hosted. Space Grotesk 500/700, IBM Plex Sans 400/500/600, IBM Plex Mono 400/500 as woff2 (latin + latin-ext for Portuguese). font-display: swap; preload the heading and body files. No Google Fonts request at runtime (RGPD).
CSS: plain, scoped per component, tokens as custom properties. No Tailwind, no framework — not needed for ~12 components, and it keeps the CSS under 30 KB.
Images: Astro's Image component with AVIF/WebP and explicit width/height. Client logos as SVG, monochrome #8FA3A8, max-height 28px.

}
Type rules. Display and H2 use the display font at 500 with tight tracking and line-height 1.0–1.05. H3 (card titles) 600. Body is Plex Sans 400 at 17/1.6; UI text 15px. Mono labels are uppercase, 11–12px, tracking 0.14–0.16em. Never below 12px anywhere.
Colour rules. Green is for the D chevron, the primary button, eyebrows and one accent per section. Green text on Ink is --signal; on Paper it's --forest. Text on a green fill is --ink or --green-ink, never white. Labels on Ink use --label-dark (Slate fails contrast on dark).
The 45° texture. repeating-linear-gradient(45deg, var(--green) 0 2px, transparent 2px 14px) at opacity .05 — only on the hero and the CTA block (there with --ink at .08). Nowhere else.
Section rhythm. Padding --s-10 × --gutter on desktop, --s-8 on mobile. Sections are separated by a 1px --rule or a surface change, never both.
04 — LAYOUT & BREAKPOINTS

One container, three breakpoints.

≥ 1024 · DESKTOP
Container 1200 / gutter 32. Hero 7:5 grid. Services 4 cols. Process 4 cols. Products 3 cols. Footer 2:1:1:1. Nav inline.
640–1023 · TABLET
Gutter 24. Hero stacks (diagram below text). Services 2×2. Process 2×2. Products 2 cols. Footer 2×2. Nav collapses to menu.
UNDER 640 · MOBILE
Gutter 20. Everything single column. Products become a horizontal scroll-snap row (see mobile design). Buttons full width, min-height 48px. Display type 40px.
Nav behaviour. Sticky, 72px (56px mobile), backdrop blur 12px over rgba(10,16,20,.88). On mobile the hamburger opens a full-screen Ink panel: links at 24px Space Grotesk, language switch and the CTA pinned at the bottom. Close on route change and Esc. Hit targets ≥ 44px.

07 — LANGUAGES

EN at root, PT under /pt/. Static, no runtime detection.

Strings live in src/i18n/en.json and pt.json; components receive a t object. The full EN and PT copy for the homepage is already in the design file's logic — lift it verbatim.
Every page emits alternate hreflang links (en, pt, x-default), the html lang attribute, and localised title / description / OG. The switch in the nav links to the same route in the other language, never to the homepage.
Product names, "Sousa Dev", "Custom Software Solutions" and URLs are never translated. Dates: 22 Set 2026 in PT, 22 Sep 2026 in EN. Phone as +351 968 378 315 in both.
Copy rules (all pages). No em dashes anywhere; use a comma, full stop or slash. No response-time promises (no "48h"); the claim is direct contact with the lead engineer. Never state team size. "Working globally", not a region. Every page makes clear we handle development, deployment, hosting and management.
08 — QUALITY BAR

Acceptance criteria. The site isn't done until all pass.

PERFORMANCE
— Lighthouse mobile ≥ 95 on all four categories
— LCP under 1.8s, CLS under 0.05, INP under 200ms on 4G
— Total JS shipped to the homepage under 15 KB
— Fonts preloaded, subset, woff2 only
ACCESSIBILITY
— WCAG 2.2 AA; text contrast ≥ 4.5:1 (already true for the token pairs)
— Full keyboard nav; visible focus ring 2px solid --signal, offset 2px
— One h1 per page, landmark regions, alt text on every image, logos carry company names
— prefers-reduced-motion: disable the loader loop and all transitions
SEO & META
— Per-page title (≤ 60 chars), description, canonical, hreflang
— OG image 1200×630 per page from the template in /og (Ink, 45° texture, headline, mark)
— JSON-LD: Organization on every page, Article on insights, SoftwareApplication on product cards
— sitemap.xml, robots.txt, 404 page in both languages
PRIVACY & ANALYTICS
— No third-party fonts, no Google Analytics; use Plausible or Umami (cookie-less, no banner needed)
— Form: honeypot + server-side rate limit; never log message bodies
— Privacy page in EN and PT naming the processor and retention
